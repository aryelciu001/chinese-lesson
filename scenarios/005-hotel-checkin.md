### Hanzi

A: 你好，我想办理入住。
B: 好的，请问您有预订吗？
A: 有，我是网上预订的，名字是李明。
B: 稍等，我查一下……好的，找到了。请出示您的身份证。
A: 给您，请问房间在几楼？
B: 您的房间是806，在八楼。早餐包含在内，七点到九点在一楼餐厅。
A: 好的，谢谢。WiFi密码是多少？
B: 密码在这张卡上。如果有任何问题，请拨打前台电话0。

### Pinyin

A: Nǐ hǎo, wǒ xiǎng bànlǐ rùzhù.
B: Hǎo de, qǐngwèn nín yǒu yùdìng ma?
A: Yǒu, wǒ shì wǎngshàng yùdìng de, míngzì shì Lǐ Míng.
B: Shāo děng, wǒ chá yīxià… hǎo de, zhǎodào le. Qǐng chūshì nín de shēnfènzhèng.
A: Gěi nín, qǐngwèn fángjiān zài jǐ lóu?
B: Nín de fángjiān shì bā líng liù, zài bā lóu. Zǎocān bāohán zài nèi, qī diǎn dào jiǔ diǎn zài yī lóu cāntīng.
A: Hǎo de, xièxiè. WiFi mìmǎ shì duōshǎo?
B: Mìmǎ zài zhè zhāng kǎ shàng. Rúguǒ yǒu rènhé wèntí, qǐng bōdǎ qiántái diànhuà líng.

---

### Translation

A: Hello, I'd like to check in.
B: Of course, do you have a reservation?
A: Yes, I booked online — the name is Li Ming.
B: One moment, let me check… got it. Please show me your ID card.
A: Here you go. What floor is the room on?
B: Your room is 806, on the eighth floor. Breakfast is included — served from 7 to 9 AM in the first-floor restaurant.
A: Great, thank you. What's the WiFi password?
B: The password is on this card. If you have any questions, please call the front desk at 0.

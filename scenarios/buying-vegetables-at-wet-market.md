### Hanzi

A: 老板，这个白菜多少钱一斤？
B: 两块五一斤，今天很新鲜的。
A: 能便宜一点吗？我买两斤。
B: 好吧，两斤给你四块钱。
A: 好的，我还要一根胡萝卜。
B: 胡萝卜一块钱一根。一共五块钱。
A: 给你钱。谢谢老板！
B: 谢谢，慢走！

### Pinyin

A: Lǎobǎn, zhège báicài duōshǎo qián yī jīn?
B: Liǎng kuài wǔ yī jīn, jīntiān hěn xīnxiān de.
A: Néng piányí yīdiǎn ma? Wǒ mǎi liǎng jīn.
B: Hǎo ba, liǎng jīn gěi nǐ sì kuài qián.
A: Hǎo de, wǒ hái yào yī gēn húluóbo.
B: Húluóbo yī kuài qián yī gēn. Yīgòng wǔ kuài qián.
A: Gěi nǐ qián. Xièxiè lǎobǎn!
B: Xièxiè, màn zǒu!

---

### Translation

A: Excuse me, how much is the cabbage per jin?
B: Two fifty per jin — it's very fresh today.
A: Can you make it cheaper? I'll buy two jin.
B: Alright, two jin for four yuan.
A: Great, I'd also like one carrot.
B: Carrots are one yuan each. That's five yuan total.
A: Here's the money. Thank you!
B: Thank you, take care!

### Hanzi

孙月：这是什么时候的照片？你真年轻！
王静：这是上大学时的照片。一看到这张照片，我就想起过去那段快乐的日子，好像重新回到了校园。
孙月：旁边这个人一定是你的好朋友吧？你们现在还联系吗？
王静：当然了，尽管已经毕业这么多年，我们还是经常联系的，每次都有说不完的话。
孙月：真羡慕你！我上大学时最好的朋友去了南方工作，我们俩已经好久没联系了。我一会儿就给她打个电话。
王静：对。要知道，能有一个真正的朋友，有一段真正的友谊，是多么不容易！

### Pinyin

Sūn Yuè: Zhè shì shénme shíhou de zhàopiàn? Nǐ zhēn niánqīng!
Wáng Jìng: Zhè shì shàng dàxué shí de zhàopiàn. Yī kàn dào zhè zhāng zhàopiàn, wǒ jiù xiǎng qǐ guòqù nà duàn kuàilè de rìzi, hǎoxiàng chóngxīn huí dào le xiàoyuán.
Sūn Yuè: Pángbiān zhège rén yīdìng shì nǐ de hǎo péngyou ba? Nǐmen xiànzài hái liánxì ma?
Wáng Jìng: Dāngrán le, jǐnguǎn yǐjīng bìyè zhème duō nián, wǒmen háishi jīngcháng liánxì de, měi cì dōu yǒu shuō bu wán de huà.
Sūn Yuè: Zhēn xiànmù nǐ! Wǒ shàng dàxué shí zuì hǎo de péngyou qù le nánfāng gōngzuò, wǒmen liǎ yǐjīng hǎo jiǔ méi liánxì le. Wǒ yīhuìr jiù gěi tā dǎ ge diànhuà.
Wáng Jìng: Duì. Yào zhīdào, néng yǒu yī gè zhēnzhèng de péngyou, yǒu yī duàn zhēnzhèng de yǒuqíng, shì duōme bù róngyì!

### Translation

Sun Yue: When was this photo taken? You look so young!
Wang Jing: This is from when I was in university. Whenever I see this photo, I think of those happy days in the past — it's like going back to campus again.
Sun Yue: The person next to you must be a good friend of yours, right? Are you still in touch?
Wang Jing: Of course. Even though it's been so many years since graduation, we're still in regular contact. Every time we talk, there's never enough to say.
Sun Yue: I'm so envious of you! My best friend from university went to work in the south, and we haven't been in touch for a long time. I'll give her a call in a bit.
Wang Jing: Right. You know, having a true friend and a genuine friendship — that's no small thing!

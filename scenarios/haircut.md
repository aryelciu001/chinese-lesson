### Hanzi

A: 你好，我想剪头发。
B: 好的，你想剪什么发型？
A: 我想剪短一点，两边也修一下。
B: 没问题。要不要洗头？
A: 要，麻烦你了。
B: 好，请坐这里。洗完再剪，大概需要四十分钟。
A: 好的，谢谢。顺便帮我吹一下吗？
B: 当然，吹造型也包括在内。

### Pinyin

A: Nǐ hǎo, wǒ xiǎng jiǎn tóufa.
B: Hǎo de, nǐ xiǎng jiǎn shénme fàxíng?
A: Wǒ xiǎng jiǎn duǎn yīdiǎn, liǎng biān yě xiū yīxià.
B: Méi wèntí. Yào bu yào xǐ tóu?
A: Yào, máfan nǐ le.
B: Hǎo, qǐng zuò zhèlǐ. Xǐ wán zài jiǎn, dàgài xūyào sìshí fēnzhōng.
A: Hǎo de, xièxiè. Shùnbiàn bāng wǒ chuī yīxià ma?
B: Dāngrán, chuī zàoxíng yě bāokuò zài nèi.

---

### Translation

A: Hi, I'd like a haircut.
B: Sure, what style are you looking for?
A: I'd like it a bit shorter, and trimmed on the sides too.
B: No problem. Would you like a hair wash as well?
A: Yes please, thank you.
B: Great, please sit here. We'll wash first then cut — should take about 40 minutes.
A: Sounds good, thanks. Could you also blow-dry it for me?
B: Of course, blow-dry styling is included.

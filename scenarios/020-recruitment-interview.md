### Hanzi

马经理：小林，这次招聘不是小李负责吗？
小林：本来是小李负责的，但是他突然生病住院了，所以就交给我来做了。
马经理：哦，这次应聘的人多吗？
小林：经理，这次来应聘的一共有15人。经过笔试和面试，有两个不错。这是他们的材料，您看看。
马经理：这两个人的能力都比较符合我们的要求。你通知他们下周一上午九点来我办公室吧。
小林：好的，那我马上跟他们联系。

### Pinyin

Mǎ jīnglǐ: Xiǎo Lín, zhè cì zhāopìn bú shì Xiǎo Lǐ fùzé ma?
Xiǎo Lín: Běnlái shì Xiǎo Lǐ fùzé de, dànshì tā tūrán shēngbìng zhùyuàn le, suǒyǐ jiù jiāo gěi wǒ lái zuò le.
Mǎ jīnglǐ: Ó, zhè cì yìngpìn de rén duō ma?
Xiǎo Lín: Jīnglǐ, zhè cì lái yìngpìn de yīgòng yǒu 15 rén. Jīngguò bǐshì hé miànshì, yǒu liǎng gè búcuò. Zhè shì tāmen de cáiliào, nín kànkan.
Mǎ jīnglǐ: Zhè liǎng gè rén de nénglì dōu bǐjiào fúhé wǒmen de yāoqiú. Nǐ tōngzhī tāmen xià zhōu yī shàngwǔ jiǔ diǎn lái wǒ bàngōngshì ba.
Xiǎo Lín: Hǎo de, nà wǒ mǎshàng gēn tāmen liánxì.

---

### Translation

Manager Ma: Xiao Lin, wasn't Xiao Li supposed to be in charge of this recruitment?
Xiao Lin: Xiao Li was originally in charge, but he suddenly got sick and was hospitalized, so it was handed over to me.
Manager Ma: Oh, were there many applicants this time?
Xiao Lin: Manager, there were 15 applicants in total this time. After the written test and interviews, two stood out. Here are their materials, please take a look.
Manager Ma: Both of them are quite capable and meet our requirements. Notify them to come to my office next Monday morning at nine o'clock.
Xiao Lin: Sure, I'll contact them right away.

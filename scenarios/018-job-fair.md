### Hanzi

小夏：你上午的面试怎么样？
小雨：还可以，他们问的问题都挺容易的，就是我有点儿紧张。
小夏：面试的时候，一定要对自己有信心，要相信自己的能力。
小雨：你说的对！3月15号上午8点在学校体育馆还有一个招聘会，你去吗？
小夏：我还没决定呢。
小雨：听说这次招聘会提供的工作机会很多，我们一起去看看吧。

### Pinyin

Xiǎo Xià: Nǐ shàngwǔ de miànshì zěnmeyàng?
Xiǎo Yǔ: Hái kěyǐ, tāmen wèn de wèntí dōu tǐng róngyì de, jiùshì wǒ yǒu diǎnr jǐnzhāng.
Xiǎo Xià: Miànshì de shíhòu, yīdìng yào duì zìjǐ yǒu xìnxīn, yào xiāngxìn zìjǐ de nénglì.
Xiǎo Yǔ: Nǐ shuō de duì! Sān yuè shíwǔ hào shàngwǔ bā diǎn zài xuéxiào tǐyùguǎn hái yǒu yīgè zhāopìn huì, nǐ qù ma?
Xiǎo Xià: Wǒ hái méi juédìng ne.
Xiǎo Yǔ: Tīng shuō zhè cì zhāopìn huì tígōng de gōngzuò jīhuì hěn duō, wǒmen yīqǐ qù kànkan ba.

### Translation

Xiao Xia: How was your interview this morning?
Xiao Yu: It was alright. The questions they asked were pretty easy, I was just a little nervous.
Xiao Xia: During an interview, you must be confident in yourself and believe in your own abilities.
Xiao Yu: You're right! On March 15th at 8am there's also a job fair at the school gymnasium — are you going?
Xiao Xia: I haven't decided yet.
Xiao Yu: I heard this job fair has a lot of job opportunities. Let's go together and check it out.

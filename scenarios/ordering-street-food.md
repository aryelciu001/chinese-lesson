### Hanzi

A: 老板，这个煎饼果子怎么卖？
B: 一个八块，加蛋的话多收两块。
A: 那给我来一个加蛋的，不要香菜，辣椒酱少放一点。
B: 好嘞，脆饼要几张？
A: 两张就够了。请问可以扫码付款吗？
B: 可以，微信支付宝都行。好了，你的煎饼好了，慢用！
A: 谢谢，闻着真香！

### Pinyin

A: Lǎobǎn, zhège jiānbǐng guǒzi zěnme mài?
B: Yī gè bā kuài, jiā dàn de huà duō shōu liǎng kuài.
A: Nà gěi wǒ lái yī gè jiā dàn de, bù yào xiāngcài, làjiāojiàng shǎo fàng yīdiǎn.
B: Hǎo lei, cuìbǐng yào jǐ zhāng?
A: Liǎng zhāng jiù gòu le. Qǐngwèn kěyǐ sǎomǎ fùkuǎn ma?
B: Kěyǐ, Wēixìn Zhīfùbǎo dōu xíng. Hǎo le, nǐ de jiānbǐng hǎo le, màn yòng!
A: Xièxie, wén zhe zhēn xiāng!

### Translation

A: Hey, how much is the jianbing guozi?
B: Eight yuan each, two yuan extra if you want an egg.
A: Give me one with egg, no cilantro, and go easy on the chili sauce.
B: Sure, how many crispy wafers do you want?
A: Two is enough. Can I pay by QR code?
B: Yes, WeChat Pay and Alipay both work. Here you go, your jianbing is ready — enjoy!
A: Thanks, it smells amazing!

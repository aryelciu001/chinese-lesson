### Hanzi

高老师: 听说您跟妻子结婚快二十年了？
李老师: 到6月9号，我们就结婚二十年了。这么多年，我们的生活一直挺幸福的。
高老师: 我和丈夫刚结婚的时候，每天都觉得很新鲜，在一起有说不完的话。但是现在……
李老师: 两个人共同生活，只有浪漫和新鲜感是不够的。
高老师: 您说的对！我现在每天看到的都是他的缺点。
李老师: 两个人在一起时间长了，就会有很多问题。只有接受了他的缺点，你们才能更好地一起生活。

### Pinyin

高老师: Tīng shuō nín gēn qīzi jiéhūn kuài èrshí nián le?
李老师: Dào liù yuè jiǔ hào, wǒmen jiù jiéhūn èrshí nián le. Zhème duō nián, wǒmen de shēnghuó yīzhí tǐng xìngfú de.
高老师: Wǒ hé zhàngfu gāng jiéhūn de shíhou, měitiān dōu juéde hěn xīnxiān, zài yīqǐ yǒu shuō bù wán de huà. Dànshì xiànzài……
李老师: Liǎng gè rén gòngtóng shēnghuó, zhǐyǒu làngmàn hé xīnxiāngǎn shì bùgòu de.
高老师: Nín shuō de duì! Wǒ xiànzài měitiān kàn dào de dōu shì tā de quēdiǎn.
李老师: Liǎng gè rén zài yīqǐ shíjiān cháng le, jiù huì yǒu hěn duō wèntí. Zhǐyǒu jiēshòu le tā de quēdiǎn, nǐmen cái néng gèng hǎo de yīqǐ shēnghuó.

---

### Translation

高老师: I heard you and your wife have been married for almost twenty years?
李老师: On June 9th, we'll have been married for twenty years. All these years, our life has always been quite happy.
高老师: When my husband and I had just gotten married, every day felt very fresh, and we had endless things to talk about. But now……
李老师: For two people living together, romance and novelty alone are not enough.
高老师: You're right! Now all I see every day are his flaws.
李老师: When two people have been together for a long time, there will be many problems. Only by accepting his flaws can you live together better.
